Source branding in the setup wizard

FnOS Video
The recognizable mark is based on the original vector silhouette from the
user-provided FnOS Video Android application, version 1.12.2. Its palette and
surrounding tile have been adapted to Aurora TV. FnOS marks belong to their owner.

Emby
The mark uses the Emby outline distributed by Simple Icons under CC0 1.0.
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/emby.svg
The gradient palette and surrounding tile are adaptations. Emby is a third-party
trademark. The CC0 legal text is included in CC0-1.0.txt.

Jellyfin
Copyright Jellyfin contributors. Based on the original icon-transparent.svg.
https://github.com/jellyfin/jellyfin-ux/blob/master/branding/SVG/icon-transparent.svg
The original contours are retained and the palette is adapted to Aurora TV.
This artwork adaptation is available under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
4.0 International. Its editable contours are ic_source_jellyfin.xml in the Android
resources. See Jellyfin-branding-LICENSE.txt for the license text.

TVBox
The original Box application icon is from takagen99/Box.
https://github.com/takagen99/Box/blob/main/app/src/main/res/drawable/app_icon.png
The user supplied this original PNG in TVBOX-LOGO. It is retained independently as
setup_source_tvbox.png. The setup view applies Aurora color grading while
preserving the separate box faces and tape. This artwork is available under AGPL-3.0.
See TVBox-branding-AGPL-3.0.txt for the license text.

The station logos used on the channel-logo setup page were supplied by the user.
They identify their corresponding broadcasters and retain their original colors.
All trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.
