Source branding in the setup wizard

FnOS Video
The recognizable mark is based on the original vector silhouette from the
user-provided FnOS Video Android application, version 1.12.2. Its palette and
surrounding tile have been adapted to Aurora TV. FnOS marks belong to their owner.

Emby
The mark uses the Emby outline distributed by Simple Icons under CC0 1.0.
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/emby.svg
The gradient palette and surrounding tile are adaptations. Emby is a third-party
trademark. The CC0 legal text is included in CC0-1.0.txt.

Jellyfin
Copyright Jellyfin contributors. Based on the original icon-transparent.svg.
https://github.com/jellyfin/jellyfin-ux/blob/master/branding/SVG/icon-transparent.svg
The original contours are retained in the XML source mark on an Aurora TV tile.
This artwork adaptation is available under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
4.0 International. Its editable contours are ic_source_jellyfin.xml in the Android
resources. See Jellyfin-branding-LICENSE.txt for the license text.

TVBox
The original Box application icon is from takagen99/Box.
https://github.com/takagen99/Box/blob/main/app/src/main/res/drawable/app_icon.png
The user supplied the original PNG in TVBOX-LOGO. Its box faces and tape were
adapted to finite-color vector paths as ic_source_tvbox.xml; no source PNG is
packaged. The box faces use Aurora teal/blue, its tape uses violet, and its outline uses slate;
the original contours remain distinct on the surrounding Aurora tile. This artwork adaptation is available under AGPL-3.0.
See TVBox-branding-AGPL-3.0.txt for the license text.

The station logos used on the channel-logo setup page were supplied by the user.
They identify their corresponding broadcasters and retain their original colors.
All trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.

OpenList logo — designed by @ubnm (https://github.com/ubnm), OpenList Team. HD remaster: @meethuthu; vector conversion: @cxw620. https://github.com/OpenListTeam/Logo/blob/a0393230a7cdba2d530f611d32755f7e40543ab5/logo.svg . Aurora TV adapts the two original paths to Android vector and web icons with theme colors. This adapted logo is distributed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; see OpenList-logo-CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0.txt.

115 Desktop
The original 256x256 icon was extracted at the user's request from the
115-Desktop 1.5.8.3 executable's RT_GROUP_ICON 1. Its real numeral
contour is adapted as a single Aurora teal path in ic_source_disk115.xml; the white
app-shell background is omitted and the contour is uniformly recentered. No PNG/ICO or bitmap
wrapper is packaged as the 115 source drawable. The original mark identifies the
115 source and remains the property of its owner. The surrounding Aurora tile is
separate. Source hashes and contour conversion are recorded in Source-vectorization-PROVENANCE.txt.

Thunder Netdisk and Guangya Cloud
Provider marks are used only to identify their media sources. Their original
trademark and artwork rights remain with their owners. Aurora colors are applied
to the reference contours; no original marketing text or bitmap is packaged.
